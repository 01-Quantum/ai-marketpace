What happens here?

  1. 1. Generate your key pair

    Create an FHE key pair. Your private key stays only with you and never leaves this workspace.

  2. 2. Encrypt data locally

    Upload your dataset and encrypt it on your machine. The model owner will only see ciphertext.

  3. 3. Submit ciphertext only

    Send the encrypted dataset to the model owner. Your plaintext data remains private.
